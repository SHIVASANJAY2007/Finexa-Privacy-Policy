Part I

Introduction, Scope & Definitions

1 Introduction

Verano Technologies Private Limited is a company incorporated under the laws of India with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Verano operates a software-as-a-service platform that helps individuals manage personal finances, budgeting, savings goals and investment planning through an AI-powered assistant accessible on the web, on native mobile applications, and via the WhatsApp Business Platform.

This Policy is drafted to give you a clear, complete and legally accurate account of our data practices. It is intended to satisfy applicable disclosure obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations ("PECR"), and the platform policies of Meta Platforms, Inc. and OpenAI, among others.

2 Scope

This Policy applies to all personal information processed by Verano in connection with the Service, regardless of the channel through which it is collected, including our website, our mobile applications distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, our WhatsApp Business Platform assistant, our customer support channels, and any application programming interfaces we operate or consume.

This Policy does not apply to information processed by third parties on their own behalf, including payment processors, cloud infrastructure providers, or Meta Platforms, Inc. in its capacity as the operator of WhatsApp, each of which maintains its own privacy notices governing its independent processing activities.

Part II

Legal Basis for Processing

5 Legal Basis of Processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases for each processing activity: performance of a contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)); compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)); our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Service, provided such interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights (Article 6(1)(f)); and your explicit consent, particularly for marketing communications, non-essential cookies, and the processing of sensitive financial categories where required (Article 6(1)(a)).

Under the DPDP Act, we process personal data on the basis of your consent, obtained through clear affirmative action, or pursuant to a "certain legitimate use" recognized under the Act, such as the provision of a service you have requested or compliance with a legal obligation.

6 Categories of Personal Information

We organize the personal information we process into the following categories: identity data (name, date of birth), contact data (email, phone number, WhatsApp number, address), financial data (income, expenses, budgets, investment and goal-planning inputs), technical data (device identifiers, IP address, browser fingerprint), usage data (in-app behavior, feature engagement), AI interaction data (prompts, uploaded documents, generated outputs), and marketing preferences.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (such as health, religious belief, or sexual orientation data) under Article 9 of the GDPR, and we ask that you refrain from submitting such information through free-text fields or document uploads.

Part III

Information We Collect

When you register for the Service, you provide us with your full name, email address, phone number, and a password or authentication credential. You may optionally provide a profile picture, date of birth, city of residence, occupation, and a WhatsApp number for linking the conversational assistant.

Additional information you voluntarily submit through forms, surveys, support tickets, or free-text fields within the Service, including any information you choose to include in messages to our AI assistant, is also collected and processed in accordance with this Policy.

8 Automatically Collected Information

In addition to information you actively provide, we automatically collect certain information whenever you interact with the Service, including log data, timestamps of requests, referring URLs, pages viewed, features used, session duration, and crash diagnostics. This information allows us to operate the Service reliably, diagnose faults, and understand aggregate usage patterns.

31 Financial Information

Because Verano provides financial planning functionality, we collect the financial information you choose to enter or connect, including declared income figures, recurring expense categories, savings goals, and, where you opt to connect a bank account through a licensed account-aggregation partner, read-only transaction summaries. See Part VII for the complete treatment of financial data categories.

Part IV

Device, Cookies & Technical Data

9 Device Information

We collect device identifiers, device model, screen resolution, and installed application version to ensure compatibility, deliver correctly formatted experiences, and detect fraudulent or automated access to the Service.

10 Browser Information

We collect browser type, browser version, installed plugins where exposed by the browser, and rendering engine details to support cross-browser compatibility and to identify anomalous or bot-like traffic patterns.

11 Operating System Information

We collect the operating system name and version running on your device, which we use to determine feature availability, apply security patches relevant to your platform, and prioritize engineering support for the most-used environments.

12 Network Information

We collect network connection type (such as Wi-Fi or cellular), approximate network carrier where available, and latency metrics to optimize the delivery and performance of the Service across varying connectivity conditions.

13 IP Address Collection

We collect your IP address for every request made to our servers. IP addresses are used for approximate geolocation (at city or region level only), rate limiting, fraud detection, and compliance with lawful requests from competent authorities. We do not use IP address data for individualized advertising profiling.

14 Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain your authenticated session, preference cookies to remember your theme and language settings, and analytics cookies to understand aggregate feature usage. Where required by PECR and the ePrivacy framework, non-essential cookies are only set after you provide affirmative consent through our cookie banner, which you may withdraw at any time through the cookie preference center in your account settings.

15 Local Storage

We use browser local storage to persist your theme preference, draft form entries, and cached configuration so that the Service loads faster on repeat visits. Local storage data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers except where you explicitly synchronize settings across devices.

16 Session Storage

Session storage is used to hold transient state, such as in-progress multi-step form data and the current step of an onboarding flow, and is automatically cleared when the browser tab is closed.

17 Authentication Tokens

We issue short-lived access tokens and longer-lived refresh tokens to authenticate your sessions without requiring you to re-enter credentials on every request. Tokens are stored using secure, HTTP-only cookies or platform-appropriate secure storage and are invalidated immediately upon logout or password change.

18 OAuth Information

If you sign in using Google Sign-In or another OAuth-based identity provider, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture as authorized by the scopes you approve during the consent flow. We do not request access to your underlying Google account data beyond what is necessary for authentication.

Part V

WhatsApp & Meta Platform Data

19 WhatsApp Authentication

When you initiate a conversation with the Verano assistant on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp-registered display name through the WhatsApp Business Platform. This number is used solely to identify your account and deliver assistant responses, and is linked to your existing Verano account where you have completed identity verification.

20 Meta Platform Information

Our use of the WhatsApp Business Platform is subject to Meta's Business Tools Terms and the WhatsApp Business Platform Terms of Service. Meta Platforms, Inc. acts as an independent controller of certain metadata (such as message delivery receipts) and directs users to its own privacy policy for that processing, which is separate from and supplementary to this Policy.

21 Facebook Login Information

Where you elect to authenticate using Facebook Login, we receive only your public profile name, email address, and profile photo, limited strictly to the permissions you approve, in compliance with Meta Platform Terms and the Facebook Platform Policy governing data minimization and prohibited data uses.

60 WhatsApp Business Platform (Processing Role)

Messages you exchange with the Verano assistant on WhatsApp are routed through Meta's infrastructure and delivered to our backend systems, where they are processed to generate a response and, where you have consented, retained as part of your conversation history described in Part VI. We adhere to Meta's Commerce Policy, WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, and data retention limitations applicable to Business Solution Providers.

Part VI

AI, Documents & Conversation Data

22 AI Generated Information

The Service uses large language model technology, including models made available by OpenAI, L.L.C. and other AI providers, to generate budgeting suggestions, summaries, and conversational responses. Outputs generated by the AI assistant are stored in association with your account so that you may review your planning history, and are processed in accordance with the OpenAI Usage Policies and the relevant provider's data processing terms.

23 Uploaded Documents

You may upload bank statements, salary slips, receipts, and other financial documents to help the AI assistant populate your budget automatically. Uploaded documents are encrypted at rest and are processed only for the purpose of extracting the specific data points you request; we do not review uploaded documents manually except where necessary to investigate abuse or a support request you have raised.

24 User Uploaded Files

Files you upload for any purpose, including profile pictures and supporting documentation, are stored in access-controlled cloud storage buckets, associated with your account identifier, and deleted in accordance with the retention schedule set out in Part IX, or sooner upon your request.

25 PDF Processing

When you upload a PDF statement, our systems extract text and tabular data using automated parsing pipelines. Extracted text is transiently held in memory for processing and is persisted only in the structured, minimized form necessary to populate your budget (for example, transaction date, amount, and category), rather than as a verbatim copy of the source document, unless you elect to retain the original file.

26 OCR Processing

Where you upload a scanned or photographed receipt, we use optical character recognition to convert the image into machine-readable text. This process is performed on encrypted infrastructure, and the source image is discarded after successful extraction unless you have opted to retain receipt images within your account.

27 AI Conversation History

We retain your conversation history with the AI assistant to provide continuity across sessions, enable you to reference prior advice, and improve the relevance of future responses. You may clear your conversation history at any time from within account settings, which will permanently delete the stored transcript from production systems within thirty days.

28 Chat History

Chat history spanning both the web-based assistant and the WhatsApp assistant is unified into a single record per account so that your planning context carries across channels, subject to the same retention and deletion controls described above.

42 AI Training Data

We do not use the content of your uploaded documents or personally identifiable conversation content to train foundation models operated by third-party AI providers. Where we improve our own classification and categorization models, we use de-identified, aggregated data from which direct identifiers have been removed, consistent with data minimization principles under GDPR Article 5(1)(c).

43 AI Model Improvement

Where our AI sub-processors offer a contractual commitment that API-submitted content is not used to train their models absent separate opt-in, we configure our integrations to rely on that commitment. You may review the current list of AI sub-processors and their data-use commitments in our Sub-processor Register, available on request from privacy@verano.app.

Part VII

Financial & Budgeting Data

30 Banking Information

Where you choose to connect a bank account through a licensed account-aggregation partner regulated by the applicable financial authority, we receive read-only transaction summaries and account balances. Verano never receives or stores your online banking credentials, which are entered directly with the regulated aggregator through a secure, tokenized connection.

32 Income Information

Income figures you declare, or that are derived from connected bank transactions, are used exclusively to power your personal budget calculations and are never disclosed to third parties for marketing or credit-scoring purposes without your separate, explicit consent.

33 Expense Information

Expense entries, whether manually logged, imported from a connected account, or extracted from an uploaded document, are categorized using automated classification to generate the spending insights displayed within the Service.

34 Investment Information

Where you input details of existing investments to model a financial plan, this information is treated with the same protections as banking data and is never used to solicit you on behalf of any investment product provider without your explicit, separately obtained consent.

35 Goal Planning Information

Goals you set within the Service, such as a target date and amount for a savings objective, are used to calculate the recommended monthly contribution shown in your dashboard and are stored for as long as your account remains active.

36 Risk Profile Information

Where you complete a risk-tolerance questionnaire, your responses are used solely to tailor the illustrative planning scenarios presented to you and do not constitute regulated investment advice within the meaning of applicable securities law.

37 Budget Information

Your composite budget, comprising income, expense, goal and investment inputs, is treated as a single sensitive financial record. Access to this record within our systems is restricted under the role-based access controls described in Part XIII.

Part VIII

Analytics, ML & Automated Decisions

37b Analytics

We use privacy-conscious analytics tooling to understand which features are used, how long onboarding takes, and where users encounter friction. Analytics data is processed in aggregate wherever possible and is never sold to data brokers.

38 Crash Reports

When the mobile application encounters an unhandled exception, a crash report containing a stack trace, device model, and OS version is transmitted to our engineering team to diagnose and resolve the underlying defect. Crash reports are automatically purged after ninety days.

39 Error Logs

Server-side error logs capture the request path, response status, and a truncated error message to support debugging. Logs are access-restricted to on-call engineering personnel and are retained for a maximum of one hundred eighty days.

40 Performance Metrics

We collect aggregate performance metrics, including page load time and API response latency, to maintain the reliability targets described in our service-level commitments.

41 Machine Learning Data

Our internally developed expense-categorization model is trained on de-identified transaction descriptions and merchant category codes, with direct identifiers such as account numbers and names removed prior to any training run.

44 Security Monitoring

We continuously monitor authentication attempts, API traffic patterns, and account activity for indicators of compromise, using automated alerting integrated with our incident response process described in Part XIII.

45 Fraud Detection

We process device, network and behavioral signals to detect fraudulent account creation, credential-stuffing attacks, and payment fraud, relying on our legitimate interest in protecting the Service and its users under GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

46 Abuse Prevention

We reserve the right to review account activity that our automated systems flag as potentially abusive, including attempts to circumvent rate limits or to misuse the AI assistant for purposes inconsistent with our Acceptable Use Policy.

47 Automated Decision Making

The Service generates automated budgeting recommendations, but does not make any decision producing legal or similarly significant effects concerning you without human involvement. Where you disagree with an automated categorization, you may manually override it at any time, and no automated output is used to grant or deny you access to credit, employment, insurance, or housing.

48 Profiling

We construct a limited behavioral profile consisting of your declared financial goals and spending categories in order to personalize the budgeting suggestions you see. You may object to this profiling at any time through the mechanism described in Part XII, Section 79.

49 Personalization

Personalization features, such as tailored savings tips and reminders, are enabled by default but may be disabled from your notification and personalization settings without affecting your access to the core Service.

Part XII

Your Privacy Rights

73 User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information: access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent. We honor requests from all users globally as a matter of policy, regardless of jurisdictional entitlement, unless doing so would be unlawful or unreasonably burdensome.

74 Right to Access

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We will respond within thirty days, or one month under GDPR Article 12(3), and will verify your identity before releasing any data.

75 Right to Rectification

You may correct inaccurate personal information directly within your account settings, or by contacting our support team where the relevant field is not self-service editable.

76 Right to Erasure

You may request erasure of your personal information as described in Section 72, subject to limited exceptions where retention is legally required, such as tax and accounting recordkeeping obligations.

77 Right to Portability

You may export your budgeting data, transaction history, and goal-planning records in a structured, machine-readable CSV or JSON format directly from your account's data export tool.

78 Right to Restrict Processing

Where you contest the accuracy of your data or object to our processing, you may request that we restrict further processing of that data pending resolution, during which time we will only store the data and not otherwise use it.

79 Right to Object

You may object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, including profiling for personalization purposes, by contacting privacy@verano.app or adjusting your personalization settings.

80 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

Part XIII

International Transfers & Security

81 International Data Transfers

Personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including India, Ireland, and the United States. Where we transfer personal information out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, together with supplementary technical and organizational measures.

82 Encryption

All personal information is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Encryption keys are managed through a dedicated key management service with restricted access and periodic rotation.

83 Security Measures

We maintain a comprehensive information security program aligned with ISO 27001 and OWASP guidelines, incorporating role-based access control, the principle of least privilege, mandatory multi-factor authentication for administrative access, network segmentation, firewalls, continuous intrusion detection, and API rate limiting.

Our security program is independently assessed, and a current SOC 2 Type II report is available to enterprise customers under a mutual non-disclosure agreement upon request to security@verano.app.
84 Vulnerability Management

We conduct regular vulnerability scanning and engage independent third parties to perform penetration testing at least annually. Identified vulnerabilities are triaged by severity and remediated according to internally defined service-level timelines.

85 Backup Policy

Production data is backed up on an encrypted, automated schedule and stored redundantly across geographically separate facilities to protect against data loss, with backup retention aligned to the schedule in Section 71.

86 Disaster Recovery

We maintain a documented disaster recovery plan, tested at least annually, defining recovery point and recovery time objectives designed to restore Service availability with minimal data loss in the event of a significant infrastructure failure.

87 Incident Response

In the event of a confirmed personal data breach, we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the competent supervisory authority, without undue delay and, where feasible, within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of the breach, consistent with GDPR Article 33 and analogous DPDP Act requirements.
